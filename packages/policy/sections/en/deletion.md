# Deletion

## Workspace

- A deleted workspace stays in Deleted for {{limit:trash_days}} days. The site of the workspace goes dark at once, at the moment of deletion.
- While the workspace is in Deleted, it can be restored.
- When the term runs out, the data is erased within {{limit:purge_hours}} hours.
- The "Delete forever" button does not wait for the term to run out: the workspace is erased within {{limit:purge_hours}} hours. After that it cannot be restored.

## Page, record, file

- A page, a record and a file are deleted at once, after a confirmation.
- If a file is used somewhere, the confirmation dialog shows where exactly.

## What is outside our control

The site stops being served from our servers at the moment of deletion. Copies that visitors' browsers downloaded earlier are outside our control.
