# Questions and answers

### How much does Markerloop cost?

There are no paid plans yet.

### Can I delete my account if my workspaces have members?

No. An account that has workspaces with other members cannot be deleted. Delete those workspaces first. You can't transfer a workspace to another person yet.

### What does the site owner learn about a visitor?

There are no visitor analytics in Markerloop. If you submitted a form on a site, the owner of that site's workspace receives the submitted data and manages it.

### What happens if I am removed from a workspace?

Your access to the workspace ends at once, starting with your next request.
