# Address and name of a workspace

A workspace has a name. It forms your site's address on a Markerloop subdomain.

## Which name you can take

- Length: from {{limit:name_min_length}} to {{limit:name_max_length}} characters.
- Only Latin letters a–z, digits and the hyphen. A name does not start or end with a hyphen and has no two hyphens in a row. Names are always lowercase.
- Names of one or two characters are not given out automatically.
- Service names (www, api, admin, mail and similar) and names resembling the Markerloop brand are taken.
- Names that look alike in writing (o and the digit zero, l and the digit one) are not forbidden, but are flagged for manual review.

## Renaming and deletion

- A workspace can be renamed at most once every {{limit:rename_interval_days}} days. The user name in the profile can be changed without limits.
- After renaming, the old name stays yours forever and leads to the new one.
- The name of a deleted workspace or a deleted account is never released: nobody else will take it.
- One account holds at most {{limit:names_per_account}} names, including old names kept after renaming. Going beyond that requires a manual review.
