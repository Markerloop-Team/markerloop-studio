# Rules of use

Markerloop is a shared backend and page editor for communities and small businesses. This page sets out the rules: what is available to you, where the boundaries are, and what happens to your data.

## Workspace

- Everything you create lives in a workspace: the addresses and pages of a site, collections and records, files.
- You create a workspace yourself: at first sign-in or with the "Create workspace" button. An account may have no workspace at all.

## How these rules work

- The rules are split into sections. Each section has a version number and the date it takes effect.
- Only the promises Markerloop already keeps are shown here.
- The texts change only when the owner of Markerloop decides so. A new version gets a new number and a new date.
